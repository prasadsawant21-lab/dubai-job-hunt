⚡ Daily 20-minute workflow

  1. Morning: Click "Refresh Daily 5" — opens fresh pre-filtered searches in new tabs
  2. Scan picks: Each card shows role, fit score, why it matches your CV, and a pre-drafted opening line calibrated to that role's language
  3. For each role (3 min each):
    • Click Apply on Platform — opens the job for Easy Apply
    • Click Find Recruiter — opens LinkedIn People Search filtered to that company's TA team
    • Click Draft Email — opens your email with subject + personalized body + CV link pre-filled
    • You click Send. You stay in control.
  4. Track: Each button auto-adds the role to the tracker so you don't lose state

Why this beats auto-apply

  • At Director level, recruiters notice spam patterns — your name gets flagged
  • LinkedIn ToS prohibits automation; a ban kills your main networking tool
  • 5 personalized messages = 3-4 responses. 50 templated messages = 0 responses + damaged reputation
  • You keep full control of what gets sent in your name
